    The Decree

    Gather at Salazen Grum

    The Queen has called you all to Salazen Grum.

    This time, simply showing up is not enough. You will be judged twice.

    Part One: answer the call

    The first battle is attendance. The team with the highest percentage of team members attending class on the day wins Part One.

    This is based on percentage attendance, not total numbers. If the Queen calls for you, you had better be there.

    Part Two: the summit battle

    During class, complete the session exactly as briefed by the trainers. This time you will also be recording your team’s performance. Your score comes from:

    • SkiErg: calories
    • RowErg: calories
    • Wall Balls: reps

    Every valid calorie and every valid wall-ball rep contributes to your team total.

    If you are paired with someone from another team, both athletes record the full score for their own team. You may also recruit a non-team member to work with you. You may complete as many classes as you wish and may change teammates between classes.

    The important rule

    For your score to count, you and your partner must be using the same machine or wall-ball station. That means the same rower, SkiErg or wall ball.

    Wall-ball weights: Men 6 kg. Women 6 kg or 4 kg.

    No splitting onto separate machines and combining the result. One station. One shared effort. One recorded score.

    Every class is another chance to add to your team total. At the end of the day, there will be one winner for attendance and one winner for performance.

    The Queen wants to know which team answers the call… and which team can actually deliver once they arrive.

    GATHER AT SALAZEN GRUM.
    All times New Zealand. Scores are saved to the shared database and update live for everyone. ♥ ♠ ♦ ♣
