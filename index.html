The Decree

The virus has killed my roses.

Pathetic.

There is no point painting them red if they are too dead to bloom. So now I want to know which team can bring them back.

Your task

Between 04:00–19:00, your team must carry as much fertiliser as possible to the top of BFT Tauriko Hill.

The fertiliser is contained in the kettlebells. The weight stamped on each kettlebell is the amount of fertiliser you are carrying.

Every trip counts. Carry the kettlebells to the top of the hill, then bring them back down so they can be used again.

The aim

This is no longer about simply reaching a target. It is a competition. The team that carries the highest total kilograms of fertiliser by 19:00 wins Challenge Three.

There is no maximum. There is no reason to stop. If another team keeps moving, perhaps you should too.

Track every completed carry carefully and add the kettlebell weight to your team total.

At 19:00, the totals are locked. One team will have done more than the rest. One team will have given my roses the best chance of returning.

And the others? They had better hope I am feeling forgiving.

PAINT THE ROSES… DEAD.
